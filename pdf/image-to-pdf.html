Frequently asked questions

Is Image to PDF free to use?

Yes. Image to PDF is completely free, with no sign-up, no daily limit, and no watermark on what you download.

Is my data uploaded to a server?

No. Image to PDF runs entirely in your browser using JavaScript — nothing you type or upload ever leaves your device.

What's the maximum file size I can use?

Because everything runs in your browser instead of on a server, there's no fixed upload cap — very large files just take a little longer to process on your device.
