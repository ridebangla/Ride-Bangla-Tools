Free Business Name Generator

Stuck on a name? Type what your business does, pick an industry vibe, and get dozens of catchy, brandable name ideas with domain-style spellings — free, instant, no account.

Type a keyword and hit Generate.

Tip: check domain availability with any registrar before you fall in love with one.

How to use Business Name Generator

  1. Type a keyword describing your business (e.g. "coffee", "design").
  2. Pick an industry vibe and a naming style — brandable, two-word combos, or keyword-first.
  3. Click Generate names and browse the ideas.
  4. Click any name to copy it, then check the domain with your registrar.

Frequently asked questions

Is the business name generator free?

Yes — completely free, unlimited ideas, no sign-up.

Can I use a generated name for my real business?

The ideas are yours to use, but always check trademark registries and domain availability in your country before launching.

Does it check if the .com domain is free?

It shows the domain-style spelling so you can check quickly, but live availability needs a registrar’s lookup — we don’t guess.

How are the names made?

By combining brandable syllables, industry words and your keyword in several proven naming patterns — every click gives a fresh batch.

Is my keyword sent anywhere?

No. Name generation runs entirely in your browser; nothing leaves your device.
