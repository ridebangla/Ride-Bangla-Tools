# Code of Conduct

## Our Commitment

Ride Bangla Limited is committed to providing a respectful, harassment-free experience for everyone involved in its projects, regardless of background, identity, or level of experience.

## Expected Behaviour

- Be respectful and considerate in all communication.
- Welcome newcomers and help them learn.
- Give and accept constructive feedback gracefully.
- Focus on what is best for the project, its users, and the community.

## Unacceptable Behaviour

- Harassment, intimidation, or discrimination in any form.
- Insulting, derogatory, or personal attacks.
- Publishing another person's private information without their permission.
- Any conduct that would reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Enforcement

Reports of unacceptable behaviour may be sent to **info@ridebangla.bd**. All reports will be reviewed and investigated promptly and fairly. The project team will take appropriate action, which may include a warning or removal from the project.
