Remove Duplicate Lines

Paste text and remove repeated lines, keeping the first copy of each.

Private files stay in your browser. AI prompts you send leave your device.

How to use Remove Duplicate Lines

  1. Paste your text into the box.
  2. Optionally tick "Ignore letter case" so lines that differ only in case count as duplicates.
  3. Click "Remove duplicates" and copy the result.

Frequently asked questions

Is Remove Duplicate Lines free to use?

Yes. Remove Duplicate Lines is completely free, with no sign-up, no daily limit, and no watermark on what you download.

Is my data uploaded to a server?

No. Remove Duplicate Lines runs entirely in your browser using JavaScript — nothing you type or upload ever leaves your device.

Is it safe to paste sensitive code or data here?

Since processing happens locally in your browser and nothing is sent anywhere, it's as safe as any other action on your own device — but as a habit, avoid pasting real passwords or production secrets into any web tool.
