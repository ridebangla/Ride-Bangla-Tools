Text to Speech

Turn any text into spoken audio — pick a voice, tune the speed and pitch, then listen. Perfect for proofreading, learning and accessibility.

Ready. Voices are provided by your browser and device.
—

How to use Text to Speech

  1. Type or paste your text in the box.
  2. Pick a voice and adjust the speed and pitch sliders.
  3. Click Speak to listen — Stop halts playback at any time.

Frequently asked questions

Is Text to Speech free to use?

Yes. Text to Speech is completely free, with no sign-up, no daily limit, and no watermark on what you download.

Is my data uploaded to a server?

No. Text to Speech runs entirely in your browser using JavaScript — nothing you type or upload ever leaves your device.

Which languages and voices are available?

The voices come from your device and browser, so the list depends on your system — most devices include several English voices and often other languages too.
