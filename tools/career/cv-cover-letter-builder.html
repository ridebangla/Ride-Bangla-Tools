CV & Cover Letter Builder

Fill in your details once, then download a clean, professional CV and a matching cover letter as PDF — nothing is uploaded anywhere.

Basic details
Photo & style

Use a clear, front-facing, plain-background headshot. Photos are common on CVs in Bangladesh, the Gulf, Germany and much of Asia; for USA, UK and Canada applications it is usually better to leave the photo out. The photo stays on your device.

International location & identity

Choose your country, then your division / state from the list. District, Upazila, area and postal code are typed by you, so every place is possible — suggestions appear while you type, but you can write any name.

Identity & work authorization (optional)

For international applications, only include identity or permit numbers when the employer, visa process, or local rules specifically require them.

Personal details (optional)

Anything you fill in here appears under "Personal Details" in the CV. Leave blank for international applications where it is not expected.

How to use CV & Cover Letter Builder

  1. Fill in your details and (optionally) add a profile photo and pick a colour theme.
  2. Add your experience, education, skills and certifications. Start a line with "-" to make a bullet point.
  3. Tap "Download CV as PDF", then switch to the Cover Letter tab for a matching letter.

Frequently asked questions

Is CV & Cover Letter Builder free to use?

Yes. CV & Cover Letter Builder is completely free, with no sign-up, no daily limit, and no watermark on what you download.

Is my data uploaded to a server?

No. CV & Cover Letter Builder runs entirely in your browser using JavaScript — nothing you type or upload ever leaves your device.

Can I edit the PDF after I download it?

The download is a finished PDF, not an editable template — if you need to change something, just update the form on this page and download again.
