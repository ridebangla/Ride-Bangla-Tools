Frequently asked questions

Is CV & Cover Letter Builder free to use?

Yes. CV & Cover Letter Builder is completely free, with no sign-up, no daily limit, and no watermark on what you download.

Is my data uploaded to a server?

No. CV & Cover Letter Builder runs entirely in your browser using JavaScript — nothing you type or upload ever leaves your device.

Can I edit the PDF after I download it?

The download is a finished PDF, not an editable template — if you need to change something, just update the form on this page and download again.
