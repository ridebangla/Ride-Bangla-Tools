Military Time Converter

Convert between 24-hour military time and standard 12-hour AM/PM time — both directions, instantly.

How to use Military Time Converter

  1. Enter a time in either the 24-hour or 12-hour field.
  2. The other format appears instantly.
  3. Use the quick chart for common conversions.

Frequently asked questions

Is Military Time Converter free to use?

Yes. Military Time Converter is completely free, with no sign-up, no daily limit, and no watermark on what you download.

Is my data uploaded to a server?

No. Military Time Converter runs entirely in your browser using JavaScript — nothing you type or upload ever leaves your device.

What is military time?

Military time is the 24-hour clock: hours run 00–23, so 1:00 PM becomes 1300 and midnight is 0000. It removes all AM/PM confusion.
