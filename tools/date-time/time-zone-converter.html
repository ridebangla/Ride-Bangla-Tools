Time Zone Converter

See what time it is anywhere in the world — pick a date and time, choose two zones, get the instant conversion.

How to use Time Zone Converter

  1. Enter the date and time you want to convert.
  2. Choose the source time zone and the target time zone.
  3. Read the converted result — or try the quick city pairs.

Frequently asked questions

Is Time Zone Converter free to use?

Yes. Time Zone Converter is completely free, with no sign-up, no daily limit, and no watermark on what you download.

Is my data uploaded to a server?

No. Time Zone Converter runs entirely in your browser using JavaScript — nothing you type or upload ever leaves your device.

Does it handle daylight saving time?

Yes. Conversions use your browser's built-in time-zone database, so daylight saving shifts are applied automatically and correctly.
