How to use Timestamp Converter

  1. Enter a Unix timestamp in seconds and click "Convert to date", or
  2. pick a date and time and click "Convert to timestamp".
  3. Read the result in UTC and in your local time.

Frequently asked questions

Is Timestamp Converter free to use?

Yes. Timestamp Converter is completely free, with no sign-up, no daily limit, and no watermark on what you download.

Is my data uploaded to a server?

No. Timestamp Converter runs entirely in your browser using JavaScript — nothing you type or upload ever leaves your device.

Is it safe to paste sensitive code or data here?

Since processing happens locally in your browser and nothing is sent anywhere, it's as safe as any other action on your own device — but as a habit, avoid pasting real passwords or production secrets into any web tool.
