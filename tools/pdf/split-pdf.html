Split PDF

Extract a range of pages from a PDF into a new file.

Choose a PDF to begin.

How to use Split PDF

  1. Choose a PDF file — the page count appears below.
  2. Enter the first and last page to keep.
  3. Click "Extract pages" and download the new PDF.

Frequently asked questions

Is Split PDF free to use?

Yes. Split PDF is completely free, with no sign-up, no daily limit, and no watermark on what you download.

Is my data uploaded to a server?

No. Split PDF runs entirely in your browser using JavaScript — nothing you type or upload ever leaves your device.

Is it safe to paste sensitive code or data here?

Since processing happens locally in your browser and nothing is sent anywhere, it's as safe as any other action on your own device — but as a habit, avoid pasting real passwords or production secrets into any web tool.
