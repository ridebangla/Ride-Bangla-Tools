How to use PDF Editor

  1. Choose a PDF file, or drop it onto the page — it opens right here in your browser.
  2. Pick a tool above the page: click to add text, drag to draw, highlight or cover content, add an image or a signature. If the PDF has form fields, fill them in the Form fields panel.
  3. Use the page thumbnails to reorder, rotate or delete pages — then click "Download edited PDF".

Frequently asked questions

Is PDF Editor free to use?

Yes. PDF Editor is completely free, with no sign-up, no daily limit, and no watermark on the PDF you download.

Is my PDF uploaded to a server?

No. PDF Editor runs entirely in your browser — your file is opened, edited and saved on your own device and is never uploaded anywhere.

Can I change text that is already in the PDF?

You cannot rewrite a PDF's built-in text directly, but you can cover it: place a Whiteout block over the old text, then type the new text on top with the Text tool. In the downloaded file it looks like a normal edit.

Can I fill in a PDF form?

Yes. If your PDF has fillable form fields, they appear in a Form fields panel above the editor. Type your answers there and they are saved into the downloaded file.

Will my pages keep their quality?

Yes. Your original pages are copied into the new file exactly as they are — nothing is re-compressed or rasterised. Your edits are drawn on top as a new layer. One exception: if you reorder the pages of a PDF that has form fields, the fields are flattened into the page — your filled-in answers stay visible, but the fields are no longer editable.
