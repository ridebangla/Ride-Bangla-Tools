How to use PDF Editor

  1. Choose a PDF file, or drop it onto the page — it opens right here in your browser.
  2. Tap any text on the page to edit it in place, or tap an image to replace, move, resize or delete it. Use the tools above the page to add text, draw, highlight, cover content or sign. If the PDF has form fields, fill them in the Form fields panel.
  3. Use the page thumbnails to reorder, rotate or delete pages — then tap "Download edited PDF".

Frequently asked questions

Is PDF Editor free to use?

Yes. PDF Editor is completely free, with no sign-up, no daily limit, and no watermark on the PDF you download.

Is my PDF uploaded to a server?

No. PDF Editor runs entirely in your browser — your file is opened, edited and saved on your own device and is never uploaded anywhere.

Can I change text that is already in the PDF?

Yes. Open your PDF and tap any line of text — an editor opens with the original words, ready to change or delete. Your new text is drawn over the original in a closely matching standard font (Helvetica, Times or Courier), at the original size, colour and position. The original words are covered on the page rather than rewritten inside the file, so they can still exist underneath the cover in the file's data.

Can I replace or remove an image in the PDF?

Yes. Tap an image to select it, then replace it with a new image, drag it to a new position, resize it from the corner handle, or delete it. The change is applied when you download the edited PDF.

Can I fill in a PDF form?

Yes. If your PDF has fillable form fields, they appear in a Form fields panel above the editor. Type your answers there and they are saved into the downloaded file.

Will my pages keep their quality?

Yes. Your original pages are copied into the new file exactly as they are — nothing is re-compressed or rasterised. Your edits are drawn on top as a new layer. One exception: if you reorder the pages of a PDF that has form fields, the fields are flattened into the page — your filled-in answers stay visible, but the fields are no longer editable.
