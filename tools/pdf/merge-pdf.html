Merge PDF

Merge PDF files online free — combine two or more PDF files into a single document — arrange them in any order, then download one clean merged PDF.

Select two or more PDFs. Everything happens on your device — files are never uploaded.
Download merged PDF
Add at least 2 PDF files to begin.

How to use Merge PDF

  1. Choose two or more PDF files — they appear in the list in the order you picked them.
  2. Use the ↑ ↓ buttons to reorder pages, or × to remove a file.
  3. Click "Merge PDFs", then download your single combined file.

Frequently asked questions

Is Merge PDF free to use?

Yes. Merge PDF is completely free, with no sign-up, no daily limit, and no watermark on what you download.

Is my data uploaded to a server?

No. Merge PDF runs entirely in your browser using JavaScript — nothing you type or upload ever leaves your device.

How many PDFs can I merge at once?

There is no hard limit — you can merge as many PDFs as your device's memory comfortably handles. For very large files, merging a few at a time works best.
