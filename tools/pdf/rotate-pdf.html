Rotate PDF

Rotate every page of a PDF by 90, 180 or 270 degrees.

Choose a PDF to begin.

How to use Rotate PDF

  1. Choose a PDF file.
  2. Pick the rotation: 90, 180 or 270 degrees.
  3. Click "Rotate pages" and download the result.

Frequently asked questions

Is Rotate PDF free to use?

Yes. Rotate PDF is completely free, with no sign-up, no daily limit, and no watermark on what you download.

Is my data uploaded to a server?

No. Rotate PDF runs entirely in your browser using JavaScript — nothing you type or upload ever leaves your device.

Is it safe to paste sensitive code or data here?

Since processing happens locally in your browser and nothing is sent anywhere, it's as safe as any other action on your own device — but as a habit, avoid pasting real passwords or production secrets into any web tool.
