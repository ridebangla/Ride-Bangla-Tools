How to use Invoice Generator

  1. Fill in your business name and address — they’re remembered on this device for next time.
  2. Add your client, invoice number and dates, then add line items with quantities and rates.
  3. Set tax and discount percentages and watch the totals update live.
  4. Click "Download invoice PDF" for a clean, print-ready file — or Print directly.

Frequently asked questions

Is the invoice generator really free?

Yes — completely free, no sign-up, no watermark, no limit on how many invoices you make.

Is my business data uploaded anywhere?

No. Everything runs in your browser. Your business details are saved only in your own browser’s local storage if you tick "remember".

Which currencies are supported?

USD, EUR, GBP, INR, BDT, PKR and JPY are built in — the symbol prints on both the preview and the PDF.

Can I print the invoice instead of downloading?

Yes — the Print button opens your browser’s print dialog with the live preview.

Is this invoice legally valid?

It produces a standard commercial invoice layout used worldwide, but tax rules differ by country — check your local requirements for tax invoices.
