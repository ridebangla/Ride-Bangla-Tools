Frequently asked questions

Is Number Base Converter free to use?

Yes. Number Base Converter is completely free, with no sign-up, no daily limit, and no watermark on what you download.

Is my data uploaded to a server?

No. Number Base Converter runs entirely in your browser using JavaScript — nothing you type or upload ever leaves your device.

Can I trust the conversion for professional use?

The tool uses standard, widely used conversion factors, but for legal, medical or financial documents it's still good practice to confirm the figure against an official source.
