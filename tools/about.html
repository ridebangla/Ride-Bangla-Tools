A free toolbox from Ride Bangla Limited

Ride Bangla Tools is a companion product of Ride Bangla Limited — Bangladesh's super-app for ride-sharing, food delivery, courier and digital services. This site is a standalone toolbox of 42 single-purpose utilities: compress an image, build a CV, generate a QR code, wipe hidden metadata from a photo, and much more. Every tool runs entirely in your browser — nothing to install, nothing to sign up for, and your files never leave your device.

Why free?

We believe everyday digital tasks — converting a file, cleaning a photo, building a CV — should not cost money or demand an account. This site is our way of putting the Ride Bangla name in front of people all over the world by being genuinely useful. The site is supported by advertising, which keeps every tool free forever.

Our ecosystem

Ride Bangla Limited runs a growing digital ecosystem from Bangladesh to the world:

  • ridebangla.bd — our main platform: ride-sharing, food delivery, courier and more.
  • it.ridebangla.bd — Ride Bangla IT: the team behind our apps, websites, design and digital marketing.
  • tools.ridebangla.bd — this site: 42 free browser-based utilities for everyone.
