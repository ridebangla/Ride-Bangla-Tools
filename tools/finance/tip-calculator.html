How to use Tip Calculator

  1. Enter the bill amount and pick your currency.
  2. Tap a quick tip percentage or type your own.
  3. Set how many people are splitting.
  4. Read off the tip, total and each person’s share.

Frequently asked questions

Is the tip calculator free?

Yes — completely free, no sign-up, unlimited calculations.

How do I split the bill?

Set "Split between" to the number of people — each person’s share updates instantly, tip included.

Which currencies are supported?

USD, EUR, GBP, INR, BDT and PKR are built in — the symbol shows on every figure.

What tip percentage should I choose?

It varies by country — 10–15% is common in many places, 18–20% in the US. The quick buttons cover the usual choices.

Is my data sent anywhere?

No. All math happens in your browser; nothing leaves your device.
