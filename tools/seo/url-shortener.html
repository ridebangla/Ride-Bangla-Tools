How to use URL Shortener

  1. Paste your long URL in the box above.
  2. Click "Shorten URL" and wait a moment.
  3. Copy your short link and share it anywhere.

Frequently asked questions

Is URL Shortener free to use?

Yes. URL Shortener is completely free, with no sign-up and no daily limit.

Do the shortened links expire?

No. Links shortened through this tool do not expire and will keep redirecting to your original URL.

Is my URL sent to a server?

The URL you shorten is sent to the link-shortening provider to create the short link — this is required for the short link to work. No account or personal data is collected.
