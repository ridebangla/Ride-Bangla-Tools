Frequently asked questions

Is URL Slug Generator free to use?

Yes. URL Slug Generator is completely free, with no sign-up, no daily limit, and no watermark on what you download.

Is my data uploaded to a server?

No. URL Slug Generator runs entirely in your browser using JavaScript — nothing you type or upload ever leaves your device.

Will this affect my site's Google ranking directly?

This tool helps you produce cleaner metadata, which is one small part of on-page SEO — it isn't a ranking guarantee on its own, but it removes a common technical mistake.
