How to use Text Diff Checker

  1. Paste the original text in the left box.
  2. Paste the changed version in the right box.
  3. Click Compare texts.
  4. Review the highlighted differences and the change statistics.

Frequently asked questions

Is the text diff checker free?

Yes — completely free, no sign-up, unlimited comparisons.

How does the comparison work?

It compares your texts line by line: removed lines show in red, added lines in green, unchanged lines in gray.

Is my text uploaded to a server?

No. The diff runs entirely in your browser with JavaScript — nothing you paste ever leaves your device.

Can it compare code?

Yes — it works great for code, config files, articles, contracts and any other text.

What do the stats mean?

"Lines changed" counts added plus removed lines; "Characters changed" totals the characters in those lines.
