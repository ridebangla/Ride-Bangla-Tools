Regex Tester

Test a regular expression against your text and see every match.

Matches will appear here.

How to use Regex Tester

  1. Type a regular expression pattern and any flags (for example g, i).
  2. Paste the text to test against.
  3. Click "Find matches" to see the count and every match.

Frequently asked questions

Is Regex Tester free to use?

Yes. Regex Tester is completely free, with no sign-up, no daily limit, and no watermark on what you download.

Is my data uploaded to a server?

No. Regex Tester runs entirely in your browser using JavaScript — nothing you type or upload ever leaves your device.

Is it safe to paste sensitive code or data here?

Since processing happens locally in your browser and nothing is sent anywhere, it's as safe as any other action on your own device — but as a habit, avoid pasting real passwords or production secrets into any web tool.
