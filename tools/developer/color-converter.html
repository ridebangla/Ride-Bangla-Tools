Color Converter

Move a color between HEX, RGB and HSL with a live swatch preview — handy for designers and developers alike.

How to use Color Converter

  1. Open the fields above and enter or upload what Color Converter needs.
  2. Adjust any options until the preview or output looks right.
  3. Click the button to get your result — instantly, in this tab.

Frequently asked questions

Is Color Converter free to use?

Yes. Color Converter is completely free, with no sign-up, no daily limit, and no watermark on what you download.

Is my data uploaded to a server?

No. Color Converter runs entirely in your browser using JavaScript — nothing you type or upload ever leaves your device.

Is it safe to paste sensitive code or data here?

Since processing happens locally in your browser and nothing is sent anywhere, it's as safe as any other action on your own device — but as a habit, avoid pasting real passwords or production secrets into any web tool.
