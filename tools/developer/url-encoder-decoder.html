URL Encoder / Decoder

Encode special characters for URLs or decode percent-encoded strings back — instant, accurate and private.

How to use URL Encoder / Decoder

  1. Paste your text or URL in the input box.
  2. Click Encode URL or Decode URL.
  3. Copy the result with one click.

Frequently asked questions

Is URL Encoder / Decoder free to use?

Yes. URL Encoder / Decoder is completely free, with no sign-up, no daily limit, and no watermark on what you download.

Is my data uploaded to a server?

No. URL Encoder / Decoder runs entirely in your browser using JavaScript — nothing you type or upload ever leaves your device.

When do I need URL encoding?

Whenever a link contains spaces or special characters (like & ? # %) — encoding turns them into safe %XX sequences so the URL works everywhere.
