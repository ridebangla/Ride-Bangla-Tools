Frequently asked questions

Is Image Compressor free to use?

Yes. Image Compressor is completely free, with no sign-up, no daily limit, and no watermark on what you download.

Is my data uploaded to a server?

No. Image Compressor runs entirely in your browser using JavaScript — nothing you type or upload ever leaves your device.

Will this reduce the quality of my image?

You control the balance between file size and quality with the settings on the page, so you can compare the result before you rely on it.
