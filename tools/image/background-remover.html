Background Remover

Remove the background from a photo automatically — an AI model runs right in your browser, so the photo itself never leaves your device.

Private files stay in your browser. AI prompts you send leave your device.

Fast mode removes a plain-colour background (wall, sheet, studio backdrop) in about a second — no download needed. If parts of the background stay, raise Strength; if parts of the person disappear, lower it.

Output size is capped at 1200 px on the longest side, so very large photos are downloaded smaller than the original.

Choose a photo to start.

If the download does not start, press and hold the picture and choose "Save image".

AI mode works on any background but is heavy: the first time it downloads about 45 MB, and on a phone the screen can look frozen for 1–3 minutes while it works. Keep this tab open and wait.

How to use Background Remover

  1. Choose a photo from your device.
  2. The plain background is removed in about a second. Adjust Strength or pick a new background colour if you like.
  3. Tap "Download PNG" to save. For busy backgrounds, try AI mode (slower).

Frequently asked questions

Is Background Remover free to use?

Yes. Background Remover is completely free, with no sign-up, no daily limit, and no watermark on what you download.

Is my photo uploaded to a server?

No. Your photo is processed on your own device by an AI model running in the browser. Only that model itself (not your photo) is fetched over the network, the first time you use the tool.

Why does the first run take longer?

The first time you use Background Remover, your browser downloads a small AI model so it can run the removal locally. After that first download, your browser caches it and later uses are much faster.
