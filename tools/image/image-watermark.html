Image Watermark

Add a text watermark to an image and download the result.

Private files stay in your browser. AI prompts you send leave your device.

How to use Image Watermark

  1. Choose an image.
  2. Type the watermark text, pick a position and set the opacity.
  3. Click "Add watermark" and download the PNG.

Frequently asked questions

Is Image Watermark free to use?

Yes. Image Watermark is completely free, with no sign-up, no daily limit, and no watermark on what you download.

Is my data uploaded to a server?

No. Image Watermark runs entirely in your browser using JavaScript — nothing you type or upload ever leaves your device.

Is it safe to paste sensitive code or data here?

Since processing happens locally in your browser and nothing is sent anywhere, it's as safe as any other action on your own device — but as a habit, avoid pasting real passwords or production secrets into any web tool.
