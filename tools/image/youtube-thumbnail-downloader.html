YouTube Thumbnail Downloader

Need a video’s thumbnail? Paste any YouTube link and grab its thumbnail in Full HD, SD or HQ instantly — free, no signup, works with Shorts and live videos too.

Works with youtube.com, youtu.be and Shorts links. Only download thumbnails you have the right to use.

Paste a YouTube link to see its thumbnails.

How to use YouTube Thumbnail Downloader

  1. Paste any YouTube video URL into the box.
  2. Click Get thumbnails.
  3. Preview each available resolution.
  4. Hit Download on the size you want — or right-click and save.

Frequently asked questions

Is the YouTube thumbnail downloader free?

Yes — completely free, unlimited downloads, no sign-up.

Which link formats work?

youtube.com/watch URLs, youtu.be short links, Shorts, embeds and live video links.

What resolutions can I download?

Full HD 1280×720, SD 640×480, HQ 480×360 and MQ 320×180 — whichever the video provides.

Is it legal to download thumbnails?

Thumbnails belong to the video owner — only download ones you have the right to use, like your own videos.

Is the video itself uploaded anywhere?

No — the tool only reads the video ID from your link and loads YouTube’s public thumbnail images. Nothing is uploaded.
