Color Palette Generator

Beautiful color schemes in one click — pick a base color or go random, lock the shades you love, copy any hex instantly, or download the palette as a PNG. Free, no signup.

Click a color to copy its hex. Click the pin to lock a color while you regenerate the rest.

How to use Color Palette Generator

  1. Pick a base color (or leave the default) and choose a color harmony.
  2. Click Generate palette — click any swatch to copy its hex code.
  3. Pin colors you love to lock them while you regenerate the rest.
  4. Copy all hex codes at once, or download the palette as a PNG.

Frequently asked questions

Is the color palette generator free?

Yes — completely free, unlimited palettes, no sign-up.

What harmony types are supported?

Analogous, complementary, triadic, monochrome and curated random — the classic designer color schemes.

How do I copy a color?

Just click any swatch — its hex code copies to your clipboard instantly.

Can I keep one color and regenerate the rest?

Yes — click the pin on any swatch to lock it, then hit Generate again.

Can I download my palette?

Yes — the Download PNG button saves a 1000×320 image of your five colors with their hex codes.
