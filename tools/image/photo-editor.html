How to use Photo Editor

  1. Choose a photo from your device — it loads straight into the preview.
  2. Drag the sliders to adjust brightness, contrast, saturation, grayscale, sepia or blur, and use the buttons to rotate or flip.
  3. Click "Download photo" to save the edited result — instantly, in this tab.

Frequently asked questions

Is Photo Editor free to use?

Yes. Photo Editor is completely free, with no sign-up, no daily limit, and no watermark on what you download.

Is my photo uploaded to a server?

No. Photo Editor runs entirely in your browser using the Canvas API — your photo never leaves your device.

What can I adjust?

Brightness, contrast, saturation, grayscale, sepia tone and blur, plus rotate and flip — all live, before you download.
