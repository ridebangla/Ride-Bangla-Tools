Image Cropper

Crop any photo right in your browser — drag to draw the crop box, lock an aspect ratio, then download the cropped image.

How to use Image Cropper

  1. Choose an image file from your device.
  2. Drag on the preview to draw your crop area (or pick an aspect-ratio preset).
  3. Click Crop & Download to save the cropped image.

Frequently asked questions

Is Image Cropper free to use?

Yes. Image Cropper is completely free, with no sign-up, no daily limit, and no watermark on what you download.

Is my data uploaded to a server?

No. Image Cropper runs entirely in your browser using JavaScript — nothing you type or upload ever leaves your device.

Will cropping reduce my image quality?

No. The cropped area is saved at its original resolution — only the parts outside your selection are removed.
