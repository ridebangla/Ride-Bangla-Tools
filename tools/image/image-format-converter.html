How to use Image Format Converter

  1. Choose an image file from your device.
  2. Pick the output format (PNG, JPG or WebP) and set the quality.
  3. Click Convert, preview the result, then download it.

Frequently asked questions

Is Image Format Converter free to use?

Yes. Image Format Converter is completely free, with no sign-up, no daily limit, and no watermark on what you download.

Is my data uploaded to a server?

No. Image Format Converter runs entirely in your browser using JavaScript — nothing you type or upload ever leaves your device.

Which formats are supported?

You can convert between PNG, JPG/JPEG and WebP. PNG keeps full quality, JPG and WebP let you trade a little quality for a much smaller file.
