Metadata Remover

Photos and videos carry hidden data you can't see — camera model, exact date/time, sometimes even the GPS location where it was taken. Clean that out before you share the file, right in your browser.

Private files stay in your browser. AI prompts you send leave your device.

Works with JPG, PNG and WEBP. The file is read on your device only.
Download cleaned photo

The photo is redrawn on a blank canvas inside your browser — that process discards every metadata block by construction, so nothing needs to be "detected" correctly for it to be removed.

Works with MP4, WEBM and MOV. Large files take longer — everything still happens on your device.

The first time you use this tab, your browser downloads a small video-processing engine (a one-time download, typically under a minute on a normal connection). After that it's cached and future videos start right away.

Download cleaned video

The video's picture and sound are copied as-is (no re-encoding, no quality loss) — only the metadata container around them is rebuilt without the old tags.

The file is read on your device only.
Download cleaned PDF

Standard document fields (title, author, subject, keywords, creator, producer) are blanked and creation/modification dates are reset — the pages themselves are untouched. An embedded XMP metadata stream, if the original PDF had one, is not verified as removed.

How to use Metadata Remover

  1. Pick the Photo, Video or PDF tab depending on your file.
  2. Choose the file — for photos, anything found (camera model, GPS location, date) is listed so you can see what's being removed.
  3. Click "Remove metadata" and download the cleaned file.

Frequently asked questions

Is Metadata Remover free to use?

Yes. Metadata Remover is completely free, with no sign-up and no watermark on what you download.

Is my photo or video uploaded to a server?

No. Photos are processed with your browser's own canvas, and videos are processed by an engine that runs entirely on your device. Your file itself is never uploaded anywhere.

What kind of metadata does this remove?

For photos: EXIF data such as camera make and model, the exact date and time taken, and GPS location if your phone recorded one, plus any editing-software tags. For videos: embedded metadata such as GPS location, device model and creation date stored in the file container.
