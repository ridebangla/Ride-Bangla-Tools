Frequently asked questions

Is QR Code Generator free to use?

Yes. QR Code Generator is completely free, with no sign-up, no daily limit, and no watermark on what you download.

Is my data uploaded to a server?

No. QR Code Generator runs entirely in your browser using JavaScript — nothing you type or upload ever leaves your device.

Do the QR codes expire or stop working?

No. The QR code is generated once and saved as an image on your device — it will keep working for as long as the data it points to (like a link) stays valid.

Can I put my logo on the QR code?

Yes. Upload your own logo (or pick a payment brand badge like bKash or Nagad) and place it exactly where you want: top, bottom, side, middle, or the center of the QR code. Two logos can be placed at once. The preview updates live so you see the exact placement.

Does a QR code with a logo in the center still scan?

Yes. When you place a logo in the center, the generator automatically switches to the highest error-correction level (H) and keeps the logo small with a clean white backdrop, so every scanner reads it perfectly.

What are the Bird, Kite, Horse and Tiger designs?

They are decorative poster styles: your QR code is rendered on a themed card with original artwork around it. The artwork never touches the code itself, so every design scans just like a classic QR code.
