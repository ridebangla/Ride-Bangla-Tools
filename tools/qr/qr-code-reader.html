How to use QR Code Reader

  1. Click "Start camera scan" and point your camera at the QR code — or upload a photo/screenshot of one.
  2. The decoded text appears instantly, with links made clickable and WiFi details laid out clearly.
  3. Copy the result or open the link right away. Nothing is uploaded — it all happens on your device.

Frequently asked questions

Is QR Code Reader free to use?

Yes. QR Code Reader is completely free, with no sign-up and no daily limit.

Is my image uploaded to a server?

No. QR Code Reader runs entirely in your browser using JavaScript — nothing you upload or scan ever leaves your device.

What kinds of QR codes can it read?

URLs, WiFi network codes, vCards, plain text, payment details and any other standard QR payload.
