Frequently asked questions

Is Wi-Fi QR Generator free to use?

Yes. Wi-Fi QR Generator is completely free, with no sign-up, no daily limit, and no watermark on what you download.

Is my data uploaded to a server?

No. Wi-Fi QR Generator runs entirely in your browser using JavaScript — nothing you type or upload ever leaves your device.

Do the QR codes expire or stop working?

No. The QR code is generated once and saved as an image on your device — it will keep working for as long as the data it points to (like a link) stays valid.
