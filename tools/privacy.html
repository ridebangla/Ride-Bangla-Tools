Privacy Policy

Last updated: 19 October 2026 · tools.ridebangla.bd

Ride Bangla Tools is designed to be private by default. Every tool on this site runs entirely inside your own browser using JavaScript — the files you open, the text you type and the images you edit are never uploaded to our servers, because there is no server processing your files at all.

What we collect

  • Almost nothing. We do not require accounts, and we do not ask for your name, email or phone number to use any tool.
  • Basic technical logs. Like most websites, our hosting provider may record anonymous technical data (such as pages visited and browser type) for security and reliability.

Your files stay on your device

When you use the PDF, image or file tools, all processing happens locally on your phone or computer. Closing the tab removes everything — nothing of your files is stored, shared or sold by us.

Cookies

We use cookies and similar technologies for basic site functionality (such as remembering your preferences) and for advertising. You can control or delete cookies through your browser settings at any time. Disabling cookies will not prevent you from using our tools.

Advertising

This site is free to use and is supported by advertising.

  • Google advertising cookies. Google and its partners use cookies to serve ads based on your prior visits to this and other websites. Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads based on your visit to our site and other sites on the Internet.
  • Opting out. You may opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google Ad Settings. Alternatively, you can opt out of third-party vendors' use of cookies for personalized advertising by visiting aboutads.info/choices.
  • Measurement. Our advertising partners may use cookies or similar technologies to measure ad performance and produce aggregate, non-identifying statistics.

Third-party services

  • AI features. Some tools offer optional AI-powered features. When you use them, the text you submit is sent to our AI provider solely to generate a response. It is not used to identify you and is not stored by us beyond what is needed to operate the feature.
  • Fonts and libraries. We load fonts and JavaScript libraries from content delivery networks (such as Google Fonts). These providers may receive your IP address and browser information as part of normal web requests.

Your rights

Because we do not require accounts and do not collect personal information to operate our tools, there is typically no personal data of yours for us to access, correct or delete. If you believe we hold any personal data about you (for example, from a message you sent us), contact us and we will respond to your request in line with applicable law.

Data retention

Any technical logs kept by our hosting provider are retained only as long as needed for security and reliability, and are not linked to your identity or your files.

External links

This site links to our company websites — ridebangla.bd and it.ridebangla.bd. Their own privacy policies apply once you leave this site.

Children

Our tools are general-purpose utilities and are not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new revision date.

Contact

Questions about privacy? Reach us via our Help Center or ridebangla.bd/contact.
