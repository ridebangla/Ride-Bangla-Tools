Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026 · tools.ridebangla.bd

Ride Bangla Tools is designed to be private by default. Every tool on this site runs entirely inside your own browser using JavaScript — the files you open, the text you type and the images you edit are never uploaded to our servers, because there is no server processing your files at all.

What we collect

  • Almost nothing. We do not require accounts, and we do not ask for your name, email or phone number to use any tool.
  • Basic technical logs. Like most websites, our hosting provider may record anonymous technical data (such as pages visited and browser type) for security and reliability.

Your files stay on your device

When you use the PDF, image or file tools, all processing happens locally on your phone or computer. Closing the tab removes everything — nothing of your files is stored, shared or sold by us.

Advertising

This site is free to use and is supported by advertising. Our advertising partners may use cookies or similar technologies to show relevant ads and measure their performance. You can control cookies through your browser settings at any time.

External links

This site links to our company websites — ridebangla.bd and it.ridebangla.bd. Their own privacy policies apply once you leave this site.

Children

Our tools are general-purpose utilities and are not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new revision date.

Contact

Questions about privacy? Reach us via ridebangla.bd/contact.
